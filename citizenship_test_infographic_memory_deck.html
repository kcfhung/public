3. "Trap" Pairs: Don't Confuse These!

Indigenous Ceremonies Protocol
Welcome to Country

ONLY performed by an Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander Traditional Custodian of the local land. Done first to open events.

Acknowledgement of Country

Can be given by ANYONE (e.g. Master of Ceremonies, meeting chair) to pay respect to Traditional Custodians & Elders past and present.

Who Leads What?
Head of State

The King of Australia (King Charles III), represented inside Australia by the Governor-General. Remains politically neutral.

Head of Government

The Prime Minister (leader of the majority party or coalition in the House of Representatives).

How Bills Become Laws
1 Proposed law introduced = "Bill"
2 Debated & voted by both House & Senate
3 Signed by Governor-General = called Royal Assent
⚡ Dual Citizen rule: Dual citizens CANNOT run for federal parliament!

5. Mandatory Australian Values Questions (Must get 5/5)

Rule of Law vs Religious Practices

Australia is a secular nation. Where religious practices conflict with Australian law, Australian law always prevails. Religious laws have no formal legal status.

Equality & The "Fair Go"

Men and women have strictly equal rights. Jobs are awarded based on skills, talent and effort, not family status, wealth or background. There are no formal class distinctions.

Marriage & Freedom of Choice

Both men and women have the right to marry whom they choose (including same-sex couples) or divorce. Forced marriage and polygamy (multiple spouses) are illegal and punishable by prison.

Freedom of Speech & Protest

Citizens can criticize the government and protest peacefully, but all actions must remain within Australian law. Promoting violence or hate is prohibited.
